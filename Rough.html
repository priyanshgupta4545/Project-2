Algorithmic Architecture

How VerifyAI Validates Answers

A multi-stage fact verification pipeline engineered to expose AI hallucinations without bias.

1
Claim Decomposition

Dissects freeform AI text into discrete, testable factual propositions while filtering out conversational hedges and subjective filler.

2
Evidence Retrieval

Extracts key entities and queries open encyclopedic registries (Wikipedia, NASA, PubMed, NIST) for primary reference summaries.

3
Cross-Consensus Audit

Evaluates polarity, numerical values, and timeline conflicts to classify each claim as verified, false (hallucination), or unsupported.

4
Reliability Scoring

Computes an aggregate trust rating (0-100%), links transparent citations, and produces an exportable audit report.

Mission & Principles

Why Verifying AI Matters More Than Ever

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs) are trained to generate fluent, authoritative-sounding text. However, their architecture optimizes for probabilistic token prediction, not objective truth. This leads to hallucinations — confident, eloquently framed assertions that are factually fabricated.

VerifyAI was created as an impartial, transparent verification layer between AI generators and human decision-makers. Whether you are drafting academic research, writing news reports, or validating technical code explanations, VerifyAI gives you the evidence needed to separate fact from hallucination.

Ethical Fact-Checking Guarantee:

VerifyAI never invents evidence or pretends a claim has been verified when reliable sources are unavailable. When evidence is missing or inconclusive, claims are transparently flagged as Unsupported.
